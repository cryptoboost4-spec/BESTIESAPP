## 📋 Technical Specifications

### SVG Requirements
- **Format**: SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics)
- **Dimensions**: 200x200px artboard (scalable)
- **Color Palette**: Use app colors from design system
- **Style**: SUPER girly, nurturing, supportive, empowering with gradient fills
- **Border**: Soft rounded shapes, no sharp edges, lots of curves
- **Export**: Clean, optimized SVG code

### Color Palette Reference
- **Primary Pink**: `#FF69B4` (Hot Pink)
- **Secondary Purple**: `#9370DB` (Medium Purple)
- **Accent Pink**: `#FFB6C1` (Light Pink)
- **Lavender**: `#DDA0DD` (Plum)
- **Rose Gold**: `#F7CAC9` (Soft Pink)
- **Mint**: `#B5EAD7` (Soft Green)
- **Peach**: `#FFCBA4` (Soft Orange)
- **Primary Gradient**: `#FF69B4` to `#9370DB` (135deg)
- **Secondary Gradient**: `#FFB6C1` to `#DDA0DD` (135deg)
- **Dreamy Gradient**: `#FFB6C1` to `#9370DB` to `#B5EAD7` (multi-stop)

### Design Principles
- **Ultra Feminine**: Think crystals, flowers, butterflies, moons, stars
- **Supportive Vibes**: Nurturing, caring, empowering, not competitive
- **Magical Girl Aesthetic**: Transformation sequences, sparkles, glows
- **Nature Elements**: Flowers, butterflies, gems, celestial objects

## 🎨 DESIGN NOTES FOR ALL REDESIGNED BADGES

### Core Philosophy - Supportive & Nurturing:
All badges should feel:
- **Supportive, not competitive**: Celebrate achievements with warmth, not hierarchy
- **Nurturing & caring**: Like getting a hug or encouragement
- **Empowering**: Make users feel special and valued
- **Inclusive**: Welcoming, never intimidating
- **Magical**: Dreamy, ethereal, with fairy-tale vibes

### Consistent Elements Across All Badges:
1. **Soft Gradients**: ALWAYS use gradients - pink, purple, pastels, rainbow shimmer
2. **Nature Elements**: Flowers, butterflies, leaves, celestial objects (moons, stars, suns)
3. **Hearts Everywhere**: Hearts should appear in almost every badge as symbols of love and care
4. **Sparkles & Glows**: Abundant sparkles, soft glows, light particles, shimmer effects
5. **Rounded & Soft**: NO sharp edges - everything should be soft, rounded, gentle
6. **Flowing Ribbons**: Elegant banner ribbons for text, flowing and graceful
7. **Wings & Halos**: Angel wings, bird wings, spirit wings, halos, divine light
8. **Crystal/Gem Elements**: Especially for higher tiers - prismatic, rainbow, precious
9. **Botanical Details**: Cherry blossoms, roses, peonies, leaves, vines
10. **Ethereal Quality**: Light particles, glows, auras, divine energy, magic

### Tier Progression (More Love, Not More Power):
- **Tier 1**: Sweet, gentle, 5-8 sparkles, simple hearts and flowers
- **Tier 2**: Beautiful, blooming, 8-12 sparkles, more botanical/celestial elements
- **Tier 3**: Radiant, abundant, 12-18 sparkles, complex compositions, angel wings
- **Tier 4-5**: DIVINE, transcendent, 20-30+ sparkles, ultra-detailed, goddess energy

### Color Usage by NEW Category:
- **Angel Wing Badges**: White, rose gold, pink, lavender - celestial, heavenly
- **Heart Circle Badges**: Pink, purple, lavender, peach, mint - warm, connected, botanical
